# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and leaders pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not just for us as individuals

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## A note specific to this project

Plumbline is a security tool. Two things follow.

**Technical disagreement is expected and welcome.** Saying "this check is wrong
and here is why" is not rudeness; it is the entire point. Correctness arguments
should be direct. Attacks on the person making them are a different thing, and
are not tolerated.

**Do not use this project to harm others.** Do not use issues, discussions or
pull requests to solicit help attacking systems you do not own, to share
material obtained without authorisation, or to coordinate anything of that
kind. This will be treated as a serious violation regardless of how it is
framed.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer listed in `MAINTAINERS.md`. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The
maintainer will respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Consequences follow the severity of the violation, and range from a private
warning through a temporary ban to a permanent ban from any interaction with
the project.
